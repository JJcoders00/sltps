Our Heritage

Founded in 2015 by Mr. Narendra Rathod.

We started with a vision to create an environment where children are empowered to unlock their ultimate potential. Today, we stand as a beacon of academic and moral excellence in Gurumitkal.

A Haven of Learning

Nestled in the serene 10-acre Bodhivruksha Campus.

Our location on Chandriki Road is deliberately chosen to keep students away from the hustle and pollution of city life, providing a peaceful environment ideal for profound learning.

Our Motto

"Sraddhavan Labhate Jnanam"

Meaning: The earnest aspirant gains supreme wisdom. We believe that true knowledge comes to those who seek it with dedication and purity of heart.

Our Mission

Empowering the youth.

To develop intellectual capability and moral excellence in students. We aim to nurture youth who are not just academically proficient, but who also emerge as enlightened, responsible citizens ready to serve the nation.

Our Vision

Unlocking ultimate potential.

To provide a dynamic, student-centric environment that fosters deep thinking, leadership, and a lifelong passion for learning.
