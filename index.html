Our Heritage

Founded in 2015 by Mr. Narendra Rathod, SLT Public School was established with a singular vision: to create a haven of learning.

We are an unaided co-educational institution proudly managed by the Sri Laxmi Thimmappa Educational and Charitable Trust.

10 Acres of Serenity

Nestled within the Bodhivruksha Campus on Chandriki Road, our school is surrounded by peaceful, beautiful scenery.

Located just 1.5 kilometers from the Gurumitkal bus stand, the safe environment and moderate climate provide a highly conducive atmosphere for rigorous learning.

Our Motto

Sraddhavan Labhate Jnanam

"The Earnest Aspirant Gains Supreme Wisdom"

Our emblem—a banyan tree emerging from an open book—symbolizes our dedication to quality education and all-round development. We explore the endless abilities of each child to make them confident, creative, and responsible champions of truth and equality.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that nurtures every child’s potential, character, and lifelong learning.

We emphasize a supportive environment where students grow academically, emotionally, socially, physically, and spiritually into truly responsible individuals.

Our Vision

To nurture enlightened, confident, and responsible individuals through holistic education.

Our vision focuses relentlessly on shaping students into intellectually capable, morally strong, spiritually aware, and socially responsible citizens of the future.
